# Tony Award Predictions - 2026

Model-based forecast built from precursor-award results (Drama Desk, Outer Critics Circle, Drama League, NY Drama Critics' Circle), Tony nomination tallies, and each show's pre-ceremony critical, commercial, and predicted-odds standing.

**How to read the columns.** *Model %* is the raw within-category score (it saturates near 100% when every signal agrees). *Calibrated %* maps that onto how often such picks actually win historically - it's the more honest probability. Tiers below use the calibrated number:

- **LOCK** (>70% calibrated): the model's most reliable picks.
- **LEAN** (45-70%): a real edge, not a certainty.
- **TOSS-UP** (<45%): genuinely too close to call - the signals diverge.

### Caveats worth knowing

- **Best Musical** scores as a lock, but the pre-ceremony consensus had it as a genuine three-way race. Treat it as the softest of the locks.
- The model runs a little **over-confident in the middle of the range** - read a 95% as "very likely," not literal odds.
- Commercial/critical/odds inputs are tracked at the **show level**, so a show sweeping the nominations lifts all of its nominees.
